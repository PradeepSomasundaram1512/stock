CRITICAL TRADING RULES FROM MARKET EXPERTS:

1. TREND IS YOUR FRIEND
   - Always trade WITH the trend, never against it
   - Uptrend: Higher highs + Higher lows = BUY bias
   - Downtrend: Lower highs + Lower lows = SELL bias
   - Sideways: Wait for breakout = HOLD bias

2. MULTIPLE TIMEFRAME CONFIRMATION
   - Check alignment across daily, weekly trends
   - Strong signals need confirmation across timeframes

3. VOLUME CONFIRMS PRICE ACTION
   - Rising price + Rising volume = Bullish strength
   - Rising price + Falling volume = Weak rally (beware)
   - Falling price + Rising volume = Bearish pressure
   - Falling price + Falling volume = Weak selloff

4. RSI INTERPRETATION (Professional Guide)
   BUY Signals:
   - RSI 30-50: Oversold recovery, strong BUY zone
   - RSI 50-60: Healthy uptrend, moderate BUY
   - RSI 60-70: Strong momentum, cautious BUY

   SELL Signals:
   - RSI 70-80: Overbought, take profits
   - RSI >80: Extremely overbought, SELL
   - RSI <30 in downtrend: Stay away, falling knife

   HOLD Signals:
   - RSI 45-55: Neutral zone, wait for direction

5. MOVING AVERAGE STRATEGY
   Golden Cross (50 MA crosses above 200 MA):
   - One of strongest BUY signals in technical analysis
   - Signals major trend reversal to bullish
   - High probability of sustained uptrend

   Death Cross (50 MA crosses below 200 MA):
   - Major SELL signal
   - Indicates long-term bearish trend
   - Exit positions, avoid new entries

   Price Above Both MAs:
   - Strong BUY zone, trend is your friend

   Price Below Both MAs:
   - Strong SELL zone, stay out

6. MACD PROFESSIONAL INTERPRETATION
   - MACD Line crosses above Signal Line = BUY
   - MACD Line crosses below Signal Line = SELL
   - MACD Histogram expanding = Momentum strengthening
   - MACD Histogram contracting = Momentum weakening
   - MACD divergence from price = Warning sign

7. SUPPORT & RESISTANCE (Critical Levels)
   - Price near strong support + bullish indicators = BUY
   - Price near strong resistance + bearish indicators = SELL
   - Breakout above resistance with volume = Strong BUY
   - Breakdown below support with volume = Strong SELL

8. VOLATILITY RULES
   - High volatility (>5%): Reduce confidence, increase caution
   - Moderate volatility (2-5%): Normal trading conditions
   - Low volatility (<2%): Look for breakout opportunities

9. FUNDAMENTAL CONFIRMATION (When Available)
   Strong Fundamentals (BUY bias):
   - P/E ratio < industry average
   - Revenue growth >15% YoY
   - Profit margins improving
   - Strong analyst ratings (Buy/Strong Buy)
   - Positive earnings surprises

   Weak Fundamentals (SELL bias):
   - High P/E (overvalued)
   - Declining revenues
   - Shrinking profit margins
   - Analyst downgrades
   - Missing earnings expectations

10. CONFLUENCE = CONFIDENCE
    High Confidence Signals (BUY/SELL at 0.75-0.95):
    - 5+ indicators aligned
    - Technical + Fundamental agreement
    - Volume confirms move
    - Clear trend direction
    - No major conflicting signals

    Medium Confidence (0.60-0.74):
    - 3-4 indicators aligned
    - Some minor conflicts
    - Trend is clear but momentum mixed

    Low Confidence (<0.60):
    - Mixed signals = DEFAULT TO HOLD
    - Conflicting indicators
    - Unclear trend
    - Low volume

11. CHART PATTERNS (Classic Technical Analysis)
    BULLISH Patterns = BUY:
    - Double Bottom, Inverse Head & Shoulders
    - Bull Flag, Ascending Triangle
    - Cup and Handle

    BEARISH Patterns = SELL:
    - Double Top, Head & Shoulders
    - Bear Flag, Descending Triangle
    - Rising Wedge (reversal)

12. NEWS & SENTIMENT INTEGRATION
    - Positive news + Bullish technicals = Strong BUY
    - Negative news + Bearish technicals = Strong SELL
    - News conflicts with technicals = HOLD, wait for clarity
