An AI stock scanner, audited in public

A research project that scans about 470 large US stocks with a language model, logs every call with a timestamp, and checks the results honestly against simple alternatives.

Not investment advice. This is a research project, not a recommendation service. It does not tell you what to buy or sell, does not know your situation, and places no real orders. Past results say little about the future. Live signals are deliberately not published here; only audited aggregates and delayed data are.

How the model's picks compare

10-day return after removing the market's move (beta-adjusted), with a 95% confidence interval. Where intervals overlap zero and each other, the strategies cannot be told apart.

Is the model's confidence meaningful?

Forward test, written down before the results existed

The hypotheses, thresholds and minimum sample were committed before any outcome could be measured (freeze date 2026-09-25). No verdict is reported until both minimums are met, and it is updated weekly.

Tamper-evident log

Every day's model decisions are reduced to a fingerprint (SHA-256) and chained to the day before, so changing any past decision would change every fingerprint after it. The newest fingerprint is also posted daily to an outside chat channel as a timestamp. The decisions themselves are released after 45 days, so this page is never a live signal feed but can be checked later.

Verify it yourself (Python 3, no packages):

curl -O __ORIGIN__/public/chain.json
curl -O __ORIGIN__/public/revealed.csv
curl -O __ORIGIN__/public/verify_chain.py
python verify_chain.py verify chain.json revealed.csv

What this proves: released decisions were not altered after being committed. What it does not prove: that a day's list was complete when committed, or the exact time of commitment beyond the outside timestamps. It is a check on honesty of the record, not on profitability.

Method and limits

  • Signals come from the live system, January to September 2026; the evaluated sample is in the summary above. Results are not re-tuned to look better.
  • Comparison groups: stocks with the same RSI, momentum and volatility that the model did not pick; random S&P 500 stocks; and one-line rules (momentum, lowest RSI, lowest volatility, everything equal-weighted).
  • Uncertainty comes from a block bootstrap over 10-trading-day blocks, since signals on nearby days are not independent. With this many independent blocks, only fairly large effects could be detected.
  • One model family and one market period. Prices come from a free data source that is revised over time, so exact figures can shift slightly between re-runs.
  • The system's stated confidence should not be read as a probability.
